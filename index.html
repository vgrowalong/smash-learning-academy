Rules in two minutes

The basics every new player needs.

21

Points win a game

A point is scored on every rally, whoever served. Win by 2, up to a maximum of 30.

2 of 3

Games win the match

At 1–1 in games, a deciding third game is played. Players switch ends between games.

ServeHit the shuttle below your waist, diagonally into the opponent’s service court. Both feet stay still on the ground.
SinglesUse the long, narrow court. Serves go to the back line.
DoublesUse the wide court. Serves must be short, and the back boundary for a serve is the shorter doubles long service line.
FaultsShuttle lands out, hits the net or floor on your side, you touch the net, or you hit it twice.

Choose a shuttle

Pick the type, then the speed for your hall.

Feather

Real goose or duck feathers with a cork head. Better flight and feel, used in tournaments. Breaks faster and costs more.

Nylon

Plastic skirt that lasts much longer. Great for practice and beginners. Flight is a little less precise.

Which speed?

Faster numbers fly farther. Match them to the temperature.

Tip: a good test is to hit a full underarm clear from the back line. The shuttle should land just inside the opposite back line.
